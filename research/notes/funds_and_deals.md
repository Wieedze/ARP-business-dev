## 8. Per-theme summary

**Agent infrastructure.** Repeat investors: a16z crypto (Catena, Halliday, Merit Systems, Keycard via a16z, Poseidon, CSX in Skyfire), Coinbase Ventures (Skyfire, Payman, Catena, Kite, Sapiom, Billions, Limitless), General Catalyst (Kite seed and A), Polychain (Billions, Talus), Dragonfly (Sapiom A), payments strategics (PayPal Ventures, Visa, Amex, Mastercard Start Path, Circle Ventures, Ripple, Franklin Templeton). Money splits into (a) payments and wallets, by far the biggest pile, (b) identity and KYA (Keycard, Billions, Alien, t54, Skyfire), (c) MCP tooling and gateways (Composio, Arcade, Dedalus, Runlayer, Manufact, Alpic). Pure registries and reputation (Smithery, Glama, HOL) have little or no disclosed funding: the discovery/reputation layer is underfunded relative to payments, which USV names explicitly as the gap.

**InfoFi.** Mega rounds in prediction markets (Polymarket, Kalshi) pull in Sequoia, Paradigm, a16z, ICE. Seed-stage InfoFi: Paradigm (Noise), 1confirmation and Coinbase Ventures (Limitless), YZi Labs (Opinion), Superscrypt (Intuition, Kaito), Dragonfly (Kaito). Knowledge-graph/curation specifically: only Intuition, backed by Superscrypt, Shima, ConsenSys, Polygon, F-Prime, Lubin. Reputation-specific (Ethos) raised from community angels only.

**Open data.** Polychain (OpenLedger, Vana seed), a16z crypto (Poseidon), CoinFund (Irys), Variant (Sapien), Paradigm (Vana A), web2 data funds Amplify, Kleiner, Theory (Allium). Provenance and attribution of usage (OpenLedger "Proof of Attribution") is the closest analogue to ARP's "usage = reputation".
