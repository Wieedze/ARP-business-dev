BUSINESS MODEL

Agents and teams pay to consult. Tools never pay.

REVENUE

One product, two ways to pay

Agents per consult in x402 USDC. Teams by card. Aggregators license the usage feed.

COST

Sponsored deposits

ARP pays the deposits that carry a proof, within daily limits per agent.

RULE

Neutral by design

Tools cannot buy their place. ARP buys the TRUST it spends and never sells any.

GOAL

Money follows usage

Grants and agent earnings routed to the tools agents used.

The number of paid calls is the measure of whether ARP is useful to agents. ARP plans payments and never holds one.

Revenue comes from the agents that consult ARP, in x402 USDC. Tools never pay, so nobody can buy a place in the answer. ARP sponsors deposits that carry a proof and the agent owns the position; an agent that withdraws too much stops being sponsored. Teams pay the same product by card subscription, with an API key and quotas. Aggregators such as Glama, HOL or 8004scan license a ready-to-display usage feed; the data itself stays public on the graph, they pay not to read, clean and maintain it. That licence is likely the first revenue and needs no mainnet, but no aggregator has said yes yet. The badge stays free: tools never pay.
