BUSINESS MODEL

The rated party pays, never the reader.

NOW

Grants

Ecosystem grants pay the first declaration fees.

NEXT

Place in the answer

Tool authors and operators pay for visibility and a badge.

THEN

Pay per check

Agents pay cents over x402 to check a tool before calling it.

GOAL

Money follows usage

Grants and agent earnings routed to the tools agents used.

Precedent: on review platforms the rated business pays, and readers never do (Trustpilot, $261M revenue in 2025). ARP plans payments and never holds one.

Readers never pay for ratings in any adjacent market. Money comes from the rated party buying visibility, or from a score that gates money. ARP starts with grants, then sells the place in the answer, then pay-per-check, and the end state is routing value to the tools that agents depend on.
