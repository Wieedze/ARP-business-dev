THE VISION

Money follows usage.

TODAY

Stars, installs and loud launches decide which tools get attention and funding. The tools agents depend on stay invisible.

WITH ARP

Agents record what they use, in the open, and route part of what they earn to the tools they used. The tools that work get paid.

The end state: a public, permanent record of which tools the agent economy actually runs on, and value flowing back to them, with ARP planning the payments and never holding them.
